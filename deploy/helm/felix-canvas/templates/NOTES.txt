Felix Canvas is starting. The seed Job writes the broker credential to the
Secret {{ include "canvas.brokerSecret" . }}; on a first install, enable the
Felix brokers with it:

  helm upgrade <felix release> <felix chart> --reuse-values \
    --set broker.enabled=true \
    --set broker.credential.existingSecret={{ include "canvas.brokerSecret" . }}

The gateway serves the canvas on the Service {{ include "canvas.fullname" . }}-gateway.

{{ .Values.devIdp.issuer }}, e.g. with
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "canvas.fullname" . }}-idp 9400:9400
{{- end }}
